02

Make it your solve

Choose your starting buffers and the techniques you use.

How it works

On non-parity scrambles with at least two more CW twists than CCW twists, or vice versa, mark the final two twists from the larger group as a 3-twist with the corner buffer. This changes the tracing output only and does not affect the estimated algorithm count.

How it works

On parity scrambles, reserve one two-piece corner cycle with different opening/closing stickers for the final algorithm. [T2C: …] counts as one algorithm; its three sticker targets are included in target counts. LTCT takes priority.

How it works

On parity scrambles with unequal CW/CCW twist counts, combine parity with one twist from the larger group. LTCT and T2C are unavailable from D-layer corner buffers. UFL forces UF/UL pseudoswap, UBR forces UB/UR, and UBL forces UB/UL, including after floating. UFR uses your selected pseudoswap edges. The marked [LTCT] twist saves one algorithm by sharing the parity algorithm. Balanced twist counts and non-parity scrambles are unchanged.

How it works

AB CD BE becomes [Sandwich B: CD] AE: one sandwich algorithm plus AE. Highlights mark sandwiches. Each saves one estimated algorithm; target counts stay unchanged. Patterns are matched within each buffer’s memo, without overlapping.
