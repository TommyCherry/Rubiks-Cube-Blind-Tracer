Choose which piece to visit first when a new cycle is needed. Choose piece mode to group letters, or sticker mode to order individual letters. The active buffer and solved pieces are skipped.

Choose helpers

Select the letters you use as helpers. In piece mode, selecting any sticker prioritizes its whole piece. In sticker mode, only the selected stickers get priority. Turning priority off restores your usual order.
